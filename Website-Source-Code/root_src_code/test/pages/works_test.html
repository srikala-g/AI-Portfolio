Works



walk down a winding path leads to the Nrityagram dance village and Gurukul where dance is the way of life. It is a beautiful sienna space, designed to nurture creativity, compose, rehearse and perform, all in the midst of an inspiring natural setting. A chance visit to the dance village introduces me to the Odissi dance form.

day I walk in on a rehearsal where Surupa Sen and Bijayini Sathpathy are in the midst of a rendition. It seems like they can dance for hours oblivious to the audience around them. The experience is visually stimulating, refreshing and awe inspiring and one can feel their dedication and passion. The music, the rhythm, the rustic ambience, the cool crisp air, the soft rustle of the leaves only accentuate the experience . Such a delight to the senses! There are no words beautiful enough to describe it.

about them strikes a chord and I feel connected. “When I dance I experience something that transcends the mundane” says Surupa Sen. I am inspired and it is not just the dancers, the dance or the apt setting, but the journey, the process and everything that goes with it that captures my imagination.


is so riveting about dancers that one feels truly inspired? Is it the performance itself or is it a culmination of elements that make a performance? A glimpse in to the dancer’s world shows you their unrelenting passion, sheer hard work, their belief in a dream and the pursuit of keeping it alive despite all odds.

move with such sure-footed ease, it's easy to forget the monumental discipline that underlies their work. “Have feet, must dance” says Bijayini Sathpathy. Feet firm in tradition and yet they push boundaries and transcend norms with intellect and creativity. Their relationship with the soil and cultural legacy, a strong connection with body, mind and soul and a holistic way of life, feels like a sign on how life is meant to be.


is my passionate endeavor to render this desire for perfection of the dancers and their graceful form on canvas, rendering timeless postures, expressions and the aura . My mind is influenced by their passion and their holistic way of life. “A walk down a winding path” represents my spiritual journey as an artist through the dancer’s life, almost an odyssey of discovery. It is an ode to the sublime aesthetics and an attempt to capture the human body in moments of sheer grace and divine harmony.


flow of this collection is like the flow of a river, starting as a little brook playing to the sound of a single string of sitar, a single color. At each turn, another nuance, another thought, another instrument, flows slowly in until it reaches its crescendo in all its colors. Art, dance, music and language all come together to represent emotions which a single medium alone cannot express. If language could bear the burden of my thought, I would not paint.
There are painters who transform the sun to a yellow spot, but there are others who with the help of their art and their intelligence transfer a yellow spot into the sun.

- Pablo Picasso
